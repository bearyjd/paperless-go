New:
* Upload queue screen. Documents waiting to reach your server used to be invisible — Settings > Upload queue now shows what is queued, why an upload failed, and lets you retry or delete each one. A banner appears on the Inbox when an upload has stopped trying

Bug fixes:
* Retry now works on an upload the app gave up on after 30 days — it previously did nothing at all on exactly those uploads
* Deleting a queued upload no longer risks it being uploaded anyway a moment later
* One damaged queue entry no longer hides the whole queue
* Offline edits (title, correspondent, document type, storage path) apply in the order you made them, even if the device clock changes
* A queued upload no longer burns its retry budget on tags it cannot read

Other changes:
* Deleting all failed uploads now tells you when some of them belong to a different server profile
